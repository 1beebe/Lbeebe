§ 05 · About

↳ sketchbook + Florida wildlife, off the clock

I've spent my career helping people learn things they were convinced they couldn't.

I'm Ligaya Beebe, founder of L. Beebe LLC and co-founder of 10xJobs.co, a personal AI executive coach built by job-seekers, for job-seekers.

Before I pivoted to AI, I spent nine years at iEARN-USA building a career in learning and development at every level of an organization. I started as the front-line coordinator helping families navigate overseas programs, moved into program management coaching direct reports and international partners, and grew into a senior grants manager working at the systems level with U.S. government funders.

Before that, I taught at universities in Qinghai Province, one of the most remote parts of China, and I was a New York City Teaching Fellow in under-resourced public schools.

AI adoption is the same problem in a new context. Most people who struggle with it aren't struggling because they're not smart enough. Nobody designed the learning for them. That's the kind of problem I've been solving for decades.

The tools I recommend are tools I build with daily, mostly in MindStudio or with Claude Code. I also run the AI Architect Program inside Never Search Alone, a free peer-learning series with 144+ professionals and zero paid marketing. When I'm not wrangling two kids, I try to fill my sketchbook and photograph Florida wildlife, and sometimes I actually do.
