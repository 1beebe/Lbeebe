Learning by building

Learning by doing now means learning by building. I tell every client the same thing: stay open to trying new tools, and give your people room to play with them. I follow my own advice. About Ligaya Beebe

I’ve spent my career helping people learn unfamiliar things. I taught at universities in Qinghai Province and in New York City public schools as a Teaching Fellow, then spent nine years at iEARN-USA, from front-line coordinator to senior grants manager.

Now I run L. Beebe, LLC, co-founded 10xJobs, and host the AI Architect Program inside Never Search Alone. I build every day in MindStudio and Claude Code, so what I teach is what I use.

The problem I keep seeing: teams get the tools but not the time to learn them. That part I can fix. Off the clock, it’s two kids, a sketchbook and Florida swamp wildlife.
