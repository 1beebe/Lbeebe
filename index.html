Illustrative examples of how a first session might go.

What I hear first

I pasted a ticket into ChatGPT once. It made up a refund policy.

Session one

We take twenty real tickets and build a reply assistant that only answers from your own help-center articles. Everyone leaves with it running.

A month later

First drafts come from the assistant, people check them against policy, and the saved time goes to the tickets that need a human.

What I hear first

Every kickoff, I rewrite the same project plan from a sales call I wasn’t on.

Session one

We turn three past kickoff calls into a template, then build a prompt that drafts the plan from a transcript, with open questions flagged.

A month later

New projects start from a draft the team trusts, and the kickoff hour goes to the customer.

What I hear first

Our SOPs live in six places, and three of them are wrong.

Session one

We pick the process everyone complains about, let AI interview the person who actually does it, and turn the transcript into a living SOP.

A month later

The team keeps a notebook of its own processes and asks it before asking each other.

What I hear first

Everything AI writes for us sounds like everyone else.

Session one

We build a voice guide from your ten best pieces, then an agent that drafts in that voice and flags generic phrasing.

A month later

Drafts arrive sounding like you, and the team spends its hours on ideas.

What I hear first

We bought the licenses. I can’t tell if anyone uses them.

Session one

We map who uses what, where the worry is, and which three workflows pay back first. You leave with a 90-day adoption plan.

A month later

A program with owners, peer champions in each team, and a number you can report upward.

About Ligaya Beebe

I’ve spent my career helping people learn unfamiliar things. I taught at universities in Qinghai Province and in New York City public schools as a Teaching Fellow, then spent nine years at iEARN-USA, from front-line coordinator to senior grants manager.

Now I run L. Beebe, LLC, co-founded 10xJobs, and host the AI Architect Program inside Never Search Alone. I build every day in MindStudio and Claude Code, so what I teach is what I use.

The problem I keep seeing: teams get the tools but not the time to learn them. That part I can fix. Off the clock, it’s two kids, a sketchbook and Florida swamp wildlife.
