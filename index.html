About

About Ligaya Beebe

↳ let's build a culture of curiosity

I've spent my career helping people learn complex, unfamiliar things.

I'm Ligaya Beebe, founder of L. Beebe LLC and co-founder of 10xJobs.co, a personal AI executive coach built by job seekers, for job seekers.

Before I pivoted to AI, I spent nine years at iEARN-USA building a career in learning and development at every level of an organization. I started as the front-line coordinator helping families navigate overseas programs, moved into program management coaching direct reports and international partners, and grew into a senior grants manager working at the systems level with U.S. government funders.

Before that, I taught at universities in Qinghai Province, one of the most remote parts of China, and I was a New York City Teaching Fellow in under-resourced public schools. I have 6+ years of experience building and leading customized professional development programs.

The problem I'm seeing in the field is that professionals are flooded with AI tools but not given the resources (time and budget) to actually learn and achieve true fluency.

The result is a team with poor morale, folks still using ChatGPT like it's a souped-up chatbot or burning tokens with poor ROI.

The tools I recommend are tools I build with daily, mostly in MindStudio, Grok Bot, Codex, or with Claude Code. I also run the AI Architect Program inside Never Search Alone, a free, semi-structured peer-learning series. When I'm not wrangling two kids, I try to fill my sketchbook and take mobile photos of Florida swamp wildlife, and sometimes, I actually do.
