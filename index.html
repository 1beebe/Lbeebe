Who trains non-technical teams to use AI?

L. Beebe, LLC is my AI training practice for small and mid-sized companies: I teach non-technical teams to apply tools like Claude and ChatGPT to the workflows they already run. I meet teams in whatever they already pay for, whether that’s ChatGPT Business, Claude, or Gemini. I do this through hands-on workshops, custom agent builds and fractional AI program leadership.

I’m a good fit if your engineers already use AI and the rest of the company doesn’t. I train help desk, operations, implementation and marketing teams hands-on, then build the small workflows or agents they’ll keep using. If you need production software built, hire an engineer. If you bought licenses and can’t tell whether anyone uses them, that’s exactly where I start.

About Ligaya Beebe

I’ve spent my career helping people learn unfamiliar things. I taught at universities in Qinghai Province and in New York City public schools as a Teaching Fellow, then spent nine years at iEARN-USA, from front-line coordinator to senior grants manager.

Now I run L. Beebe, LLC, co-founded 10xJobs, and host the AI Architect Program inside Never Search Alone. I build every day in MindStudio, Claude Code, and Codex, so what I teach is what I use.

The problem I keep seeing: teams get the tools but not the time to learn them. That part I can fix. Off the clock, it’s two kids, a sketchbook and Florida swamp wildlife.
